Potential issues with GammaMax in the context of database error correction:
- Inability to capture relationships between rows, leading to inability to fix NULL or missing values.
- Oracle definition becomes difficult for names and numeric values.
- Constructing initial DFA becomes very difficult for high number of unique positive examples.
- Unable to learn the context of numerical values
- Lack of negative examples unless neighborhood exploration is implemented.

Potential avoidance:
- Select or use specific datasets without these problems (seems shady).
- Expand the GammaMax algorithm (very complex).
